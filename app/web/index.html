Debris survey for ROV cleanup crews

Turn dive footage into a debris inventory you can stand behind.

Upload an ROV video or a batch of stills. AquaGuard counts tyres, nets, bottles, cans and bags frame by frame, sends the uncertain boxes to a person for a quick yes or no, and hands you a report for the funder and a corrections file for the next model.

How a mission works

01 · Upload

One dive, one mission

A video is sampled every N seconds; stills are taken as they are. Frames are kept at 1280 px so reviewers see what the model saw.

02 · Review

People settle the doubtful boxes

Boxes below the auto-count threshold are not counted until someone confirms them. Keyboard review: Y, N, 1–6 to relabel, A to add a missed item.

03 · Report

Inventory, timeline, evidence

Counts by class, a per-frame timeline, hotspot frames and the counting rules, as HTML or PDF. Plus a YOLO-format corrections bundle for retraining.

Start a mission from a script

curl -X POST http://localhost:8000/api/v1/missions \
  -H "X-API-Key: $AQUAGUARD_KEY" \
  -F files=@dive.mp4 -F name="Harbour wall T3" \
  -F every_s=2

curl -H "X-API-Key: $AQUAGUARD_KEY" \
  http://localhost:8000/api/v1/missions/<id>/report.pdf -o report.pdf

Ask about a single frame

curl -X POST http://localhost:8000/api/v1/reason \
  -H "X-API-Key: $AQUAGUARD_KEY" \
  -F file=@frame.jpg \
  -F question="How many tyres are there?"

# Refuses when the boxes can't support an answer:
# depth, weight, brand, or low confidence.
